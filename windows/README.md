# CH03 DEMO for Windows

A little retro TV casino that sits on your desktop. Fictional coins only: no real money, ever.

## Download (pick one)

- **Installer (recommended):** [CH03-DEMO-0.9.1-setup.exe](https://github.com/ibarrett50cal/ch03-demo/releases/download/v0.9.1/CH03-DEMO-0.9.1-setup.exe) (113 MB)
- **No install:** [CH03-DEMO-0.9.1-win.zip](https://github.com/ibarrett50cal/ch03-demo/releases/download/v0.9.1/CH03-DEMO-0.9.1-win.zip) (155 MB)

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

**If your browser warns about the download** ("isn't commonly downloaded"): that's only because the game is new.
In **Edge**: point at the download → **...** → **Keep** → **Show more** → **Keep anyway**. In **Chrome**: click
**Keep** (or **Download suspicious file**).

## Install it

1. Double-click **CH03-DEMO-0.9.1-setup.exe**.
2. **If a blue box says "Windows protected your PC":** click the small **More info** link under the text, then the
   **Run anyway** button that appears. (Windows says this about any new game that isn't signed by a big company yet.
   It only asks the first time. Nothing is wrong with the game.)
3. Click through the installer. CH03 DEMO starts, and a shortcut goes on your desktop.

## Or, without installing

1. Right-click **CH03-DEMO-0.9.1-win.zip** → **Extract All...** → **Extract**.
2. Open the new folder and double-click **CH03 DEMO.exe**.
3. **If a blue box says "Windows protected your PC":** click the small **More info** link, then **Run anyway**.

## Playing

- Click the screen to start. Drag the handle on top to move the TV; drag its top-left corner to resize it.
- The book on the stack is the instruction manual.
- To quit: the little TV icon by the clock → **Quit**, or MENU → QUIT.

**Your progress comes with you:** when you get the full game on the same PC, it offers to carry your demo save over.

**Remove it:** Settings → Apps → **CH03 DEMO** → Uninstall (or just delete the folder, if you used the .zip).
